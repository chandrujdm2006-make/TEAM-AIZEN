2. Turn Detection Algorithm

The backend queries the ordered sequence of route points. As the rider moves, Haversine geodesic distance computes proximity to the upcoming turn maneuver. When proximity drops below 35m, the HUD seamlessly transitions to the next turn step, providing distraction-free visual cues with distance countdown.

3. End-to-End IoT Data Flow

[NEO-6M GPS Module]
  ↓ (NMEA Sentences over UART)
[ESP32-C3 Microcontroller]
  ↓ (HTTP POST /api/devices/:id/location via Wi-Fi)
[Node.js Express + SQLite Server]
  ↓ (Calculates Next Turn Instruction & Distance)
  ↓ (Broadcasts via Socket.IO WebSocket)
[Companion Web App Dashboard & OLED Simulator]

4. Safety Failsafes

  • Proximity Override: Ultrasonic sensor readings under 100cm instantly supersede navigation to show a flashing hazard warning.
  • One-Touch SOS: Pressing the physical handlebar button immediately dispatches GPS coordinates to emergency guardians.
  • Zero Billing Dependency: Runs anywhere locally with mock fallback, free OSRM, or Google Maps.
